Who's playing?

Answer questions to earn spins, then stop the board. Points add up. Hit the Raptor and your score goes to zero.

How to play

Up to 7 contestants play on one screen. Each human player picks a buzzer key during setup (1–7 by default) and uses it, or the button on their own podium, to spin, stop the board and keep spinning. Other players' keys do nothing on your turn.

Each round starts with a question round: every contestant gets one question about Children's Miracle Network, ModSquad or gaming terms. A right answer earns 3 spins, a wrong one earns 1. On the board, the contestant with the fewest points spins first. Points and prizes add to your score; squares marked "+one spin" also give you another spin. Land on the Raptor and your score drops to 0. Four Raptors and you're out.

After any spin you can keep spinning or pass all your remaining spins to the next player in line. Passed spins must be played; they can't be passed back. Round 2 has bigger values. Most points after Round 2 wins.
